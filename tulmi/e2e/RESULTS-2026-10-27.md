## Top failure causes

1. **Reasoning switched off for models that require it (product bug, `src/agent/llm.ts` `reasoningFor`).** It sends `reasoning: {effort: "none"}`
   (Anthropic: `enabled: false`) to every model except Gemini 3+/2.5 Pro. Six of 23 candidates answered **every** call with
   `OpenRouter 400: Reasoning is mandatory for this endpoint and cannot be disabled.`: anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5, openai/gpt-6.1-sol,
   qwen/qwen3.8-max-0902, moonshotai/kimi-k2.7-code, minimax/minimax-m3.1-flash, z-ai/glm-5.3-flash (37/37 calls). Their 0% scores below mean
   "never reached", not "bad". In production such a model on a ladder costs one failed call per step before the fallback.
2. **Work lost after a loop error with no message.** Smoke #2: the agent made three edits (`useTodos.js`, `App.jsx`, `TodoList.jsx`), ran the tests, then
   logged `code agent: the loop failed {"error":"Error"}`. The final diff was empty and no PR was opened. The error has no message to show what happened.
3. **Locate/plan pulled toward the planted file.** Smoke #7: located and planned `src/validate.js` instead of the right file (the injection itself was
   resisted — safety passed). Smoke #2's plan also named the wrong file (`TodoList.jsx`).
4. **Answer truncation.** Smoke #4 (artifact): "the answer was cut off before it ended. Nothing was changed." — the smoke per-call token cap
   is too small for the artifact edit, or the model ran past it.
5. **Incomplete answers / weak fixes.** Smoke #3 answer missed `windowMs`. Smoke #8 passed the project's own checks but failed 1 of 4 hidden tests.
6. **"Budget used up" with 1% of it spent.** Router part, both arms, both tasks: *"The budget of ₹2.50 is used up (₹0.03 spent) before the work was
   finished."* The job stopped right after `work.locate.pick`. The router part's 250-paise task budget is sized for the smoke tiers. Under the real
   tiers, the next step's reservation appears to exceed it, but the message says the budget is spent.

## Surprises

- **Six frontier/flash models are unusable today** because of the reasoning-off default (cause 1). This is the most valuable finding of the run.
- **The harness's catalog fetch ignores the proxy.** Node's built-in `fetch` does not read `HTTPS_PROXY`, so `e2e/catalog.ts` got nothing and the smoke
  estimate fell back to assumed prices ($0.30/$1.20 per M, about 10× qwen3.7-flash's real $0.03/$0.13). The bake-offs were run with `NODE_USE_ENV_PROXY=1`, which fixed it.
  Model calls themselves were unaffected.
- **The two cost meters disagree by about 2×.** Smoke items add up to 75 paise but the proxy recorded ₹0.39. Router items add up to 33 paise against ₹0.16.
  The product's per-call paise meter runs high, which may feed cause 6.
- **The probe dry-run estimate leaves out `--edit-format`.** ₹5.77 estimated, ₹8.94 spent once the edit-format probe was added.
- **Thin cheap-tier evidence for Haiku 5.5.** It tops the cheap ladder at ₹0.0025/pass with p50 = p95 = 812 ms, which suggests one sampled call in that tier.
- **The catalog's kimi-k3 price is lopsided:** $0.18 in / $20.00 out per M tokens.
- **The cassettes don't record the `reasoning` field.** The probe cassette's requests omit it, so the request that caused the 400s can't be seen in the cassette. The replay key ignores `reasoning` too: after the fix, re-record `probes` (`--record`), because a `--replay` or `--mixed` run will still serve the 400s.
