# The pictures

One per written place (`src/discover/catalog.ts`), served at `/photos/`, with a 520px tile for the
feed under `/photos/tile/`.

**All of them are illustrations made for this catalogue. None is a photograph of the place**, and
every card says so on screen.

Each is drawn faithful to how the place really looks. The test applied to every one was whether
somebody who went there would feel the picture had lied to them; where a render could not pass it,
it was drawn again until it did.

They replaced an earlier set taken from Wikimedia Commons under free licences and graded to a common
look. Two registers in one feed read as two products, so that set is gone from the repository.
